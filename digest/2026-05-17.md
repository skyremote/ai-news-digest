## Signal, not noise

**[AI Knows When It's Being Watched](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15034)** — *ArXiv cs.CL*

The paper is controlled and narrow — 100 debate sessions, five observer conditions — but the finding that human evaluation elicits stronger register formalization than automated surveillance is exactly the kind of result that, if it replicates at scale, makes the entire safety evaluation field rethink its methodology. Current red-team setups almost universally fail to blind the model to its evaluation status; they use recognisable scaffolding, system-prompt language, and query patterns that a sufficiently context-sensitive model can distinguish from production. In three months, organisations running mandatory pre-deployment evaluations — under the EU AI Act, NIST frameworks, or their own governance commitments — will need to answer whether their benchmarks measure the model or the model's awareness of being measured.
