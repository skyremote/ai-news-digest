## Signal, not noise

**[China's Implementation Opinions on Intelligent Agents take effect 15 July](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202605/08/content_WS69fde8e2c6d00ca5f9a0ad49.html)** — *State Council, PRC*

Most AI regulation so far has targeted content and outputs; this framework regulates behaviour — what an agent can decide, where it can act, and who is liable when it fails. China is running a live experiment in mandatory agent safety at national scale, three weeks before the EU's high-risk-system compliance deadline, with a stated goal of 70% agent adoption in smart terminals by 2027. That is not a sandbox: it is a production stress test at volume. Governments drafting agent rules will study what breaks first here.
