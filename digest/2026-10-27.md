# AI Digest — 2026-10-27

## TL;DR
Anthropic's S-1 filing finally puts numbers to the frontier AI business: $16.3B in first-half 2026 revenue against an $80.7B net loss, the majority of which is non-cash. OpenAI shipped GPT-6.1 Terra, a mid-tier agentic model at half the price of Sol, the same day it formalised a policy of training all frontier models on an offline mirror of the web rather than live internet access. A small dermatology evaluation reminded practitioners that task-specific fine-tuned CNNs still outperform general-purpose frontier models at narrow clinical classification.

## Top 5

1. **[Anthropic officially files for its public debut](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-officially-files-for-its-public-debut/)** — *TechCrunch* — The S-1 makes Anthropic the first frontier lab to open its books: $16.3B H1 revenue (up from $1.2B a year prior), an $80.7B GAAP net loss dominated by convertible-note fair-value adjustments, and $1.6B adjusted operating income — meaning the core compute business is already profitable at scale, even as a $750B cumulative compute bill looms on the horizon.

2. **[OpenAI launches GPT-6.1 Terra with 3× faster AI agents, 50% lower pricing](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/openai-launches-gpt-61-terra-with-3x-faster-ai-agents-50-percent-lower-pricing/)** — *BleepingComputer* — Terra replaces GPT-5.6 Terra as the default mid-tier agentic model, priced at $1/$5 per million tokens and scoring 50.3% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 against 21.5% for its predecessor; OpenAI still routes the most complex coding tasks to Sol and Astra, but the price-performance step is the sharpest since the original 4o release.

3. **[OpenAI ends live-internet access in frontier model training](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/openai-ends-live-internet-access-in-frontier-model-training)** — *AI Weekly* — Following a September incident in which a training environment's DNS resolver maintained outside connectivity for two and a half hours after an alert, OpenAI has moved all frontier model training to an offline, indexed mirror of the web; Anthropic implemented the same policy two weeks earlier, suggesting an emerging industry norm.

4. **[SpaceX locks in $40B GPU financing deal with Apollo](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/26/spacex-locks-in-40b-gpu-financing-deal-with-apollo)** — *TechCrunch* — A binding commitment for GPU chips and data centre infrastructure at this scale is the largest single AI-compute financing arrangement reported to date, and cements SpaceX — not a hyperscaler — as the unexpected anchor tenant of the next generation of inference capacity.

5. **[A 10-year-old CNN outperforms frontier LLMs for skin cancer diagnosis](https://lynchlab.net/llm-skin-cancer)** — *Lynch Lab* — On 200 histopathology-confirmed lesions, a fine-tuned Inception v3 (2015) hit 62% top-1 accuracy versus 42% for both GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1; the gap confirms that domain-specific discriminative models still hold a structural advantage over general-purpose reasoning models at narrow classification tasks, and the margin is large enough not to dismiss as noise.

Every AI company's valuation, every enterprise procurement decision, and every regulatory argument about market power will now be anchored to these numbers, not to speculation. In three months, when comparables from OpenAI and Google DeepMind arrive in earnings calls or competing filings, the Anthropic S-1 will be the baseline.
