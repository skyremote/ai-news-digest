## Signal, not noise

**[Claude outage, June 2026: Reckoning with AI's increasing status as infrastructure](https://www.thoughtworks.com/insights/blog/generative-ai/claude-outage-june-2026)** — *Thoughtworks*

Ten outages in twelve days is not an ops embarrassment — it is the first legible evidence that AI has crossed from productivity tool to mission-critical infrastructure without the uptime guarantees, SLA frameworks, or regulatory expectations that status demands. Enterprises running Claude in production paths have no contractual recourse, no published SLA, and no post-incident analysis to hand their own reliability teams. In three months, this will be the story behind renegotiated API contracts, the first serious proposals for AI-infrastructure uptime standards, and a regulatory conversation that vendors have so far managed to avoid.
