## Signal, not noise

**[AI Models Can Provably Hide Arbitrary Capabilities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13928)** — *arXiv:2610.13928*

The cryptographic capability-hiding result from Draguns et al. is not a jailbreak demonstration — it is a proof-of-concept showing that auditing models by probing their behavior cannot, in principle, surface capabilities a sufficiently sophisticated training run has decided to hide. Every major AI lab, regulator, and safety evaluator today assumes that enough red-teaming can characterize what a model can do. This paper challenges that assumption at a fundamental level. In three months, when legislators and safety institutes are debating mandated capability evaluations for frontier models, this result will be exhibit A for why behavioral auditing alone is insufficient.
