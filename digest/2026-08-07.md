# AI News Digest — 2026-08-07

**Executive summary.** Alibaba released Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter MoE model with open weights due next week, compressing the gap between frontier capability and commodity access faster than most expected. The EU AI Act's transparency obligations went live on August 2nd, giving the European AI Office real enforcement teeth over both deployers and general-purpose model providers. Meta, meanwhile, is facing a credibility crisis after a Wired investigation found it ran AI-generated CSAM ads for nine months across its properties, surviving direct notification to the company.

---

**1. Alibaba Qwen3.8-Max: 2.4T-parameter MoE at aggressive pricing**

Alibaba released Qwen3.8-Max on August 3rd — 2.4 trillion total parameters, 95 billion active at inference, 1M-token context window, available via API at $2/$6 per million input/output tokens. Open weights for a model at this scale will release the week of August 10th, the first time Alibaba has open-sourced at this tier. Benchmarks show it rivalling Claude Sonnet 5 on most tasks and falling only below Fable 5 at the frontier.

*Take: The open-weights drop is the story. A locally-runnable 95B-active-parameter model at this quality level rewrites the calculus on what you need an API contract for.*

*— [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-03/alibaba-drops-another-china-ai-model-with-breakthrough-performance), [TNGlobal](https://technode.global/2026/08/04/chinas-alibaba-launches-qwen3-8-max-ai-model-with-2-4t-parameters-1m-token-context-window/)*

**2. EU AI Act transparency rules: now enforced, not pending**

Since August 2nd, EU law requires chatbots to identify themselves as AI, deepfakes to carry machine-readable watermarks, and providers of general-purpose AI models to submit to European AI Office investigations, model access requests, and recall orders. Fines go up to the higher of €15M or 3% of global annual turnover. High-risk AI rules were deferred to December 2027, but the transparency layer and GPAI enforcement are not drafts — they are live.

*Take: Any product interacting with EU users now has a disclosure obligation. If you haven't audited your chatbot UX against Article 50, the window to do so quietly has closed.*

*— [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august), [Wilson Sonsini](https://www.wsgr.com/en/insights/eu-ai-act-enforcement-phase-begins.html)*

**3. Meta ran AI-generated CSAM ads for nine months**

A Wired investigation, published August 6th, found that the Tech Transparency Project identified more than 50 paid ads containing explicit AI-generated child sexual abuse imagery that ran across Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and Messenger between November 2025 and early August 2026. Some ads remained live after Meta was directly notified. The ads reached thousands of accounts in the US, UK, and more than a dozen European countries.

*Take: This is not a moderation failure at the margin — it is a systematic breakdown of ad review for a category of content Meta has had a decade to get right. EU enforcement arriving the same week is not good timing for them.*

*— [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2231100/meta-apps-displayed-ads-that-contained-ai-generated-csam/), [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/08/223-meta-ai-generated-csam-ads-india-investigation/)*

**4. OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 Luna by 80%**

On July 30th, three weeks after the GPT-5.6 launch, OpenAI dropped Luna pricing from $1/$6 to $0.20/$1.20 per million input/output tokens — an 80% reduction. Terra was cut 20%. The move arrived one day before Qwen3.8-Max's API went live.

*Take: Frontier mid-tier pricing is collapsing. If you locked in cost assumptions based on Luna's launch price for anything running at scale, revise them now.*

*— [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/openai-just-cut-gpt-5-013753910.html)*

---

**5. Simon Willison releases LLM 0.32**

LLM 0.32, released August 4th, is the most significant update to Willison's CLI/Python library since launch. It streams reasoning traces to stderr, uses the OpenAI Responses API for interleaved reasoning and tool calls, exposes provider-hosted tools (WebSearch and CodeInterpreter for OpenAI models; WebSearch, WebFetch, CodeExecution, and AnthropicMCP for Claude), and replaces the log store with a content-addressed SQLite backend. GPT-5.6 Luna is now the default model.

*Take: If you are building multi-model pipelines in Python, this release removes several integration layers you were writing by hand.*

*— [simonwillison.net](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Aug/4/new-release-of-llm/)*

## Signal-not-noise

**Power, not chips, is the constraint now — and the clock is ticking.**

FERC voted on June 18th to order the six largest US grid operators to explain, within 60 days, how they will connect AI data centers faster. That deadline falls mid-August. The gap between AI compute demand and grid delivery capacity has moved from a planning problem to a structural limit: Microsoft's Three Mile Island restart (835 MW) and Meta's Clinton, Illinois plant deal (1,100 MW) signal that hyperscalers are no longer buying power, they are becoming power companies. The FERC filings due this month will set interconnection rules that determine where the next generation of data centers can physically be built.

*— [tech-insider.org](https://tech-insider.org/ferc-grid-order-ai-data-center-power-2026/), [Enline](https://enline.energy/articles/ai-data-center-grid-capacity-2026)*
