## Signal, not noise

**[Anthropic cleared to restore Mythos 5 access to certain US organisations](https://www.euronews.com/2026/06/27/anthropic-cleared-to-restore-mythos-5-access-to-certain-us-organisations)** — *Euronews*

The mechanism behind the June 12 Fable 5 shutdown is what matters: a BIS "Is Informed" letter under ECRA, which carries no public comment period, no advance notice to the company, and no formal rulemaking. Anthropic had three days between launch and global suspension. The June 27 partial restoration is governed by a named annex restricting access to specific vetted US entities — not a general unlock. The playbook is now demonstrated: the U.S. government can impose a kill switch on any domestic frontier model's international availability on short notice, using an existing legal mechanism that predates AI entirely. Expect other governments to develop equivalent tools, and expect export-control provisions to start appearing in enterprise AI contracts and model licensing terms within the quarter.
