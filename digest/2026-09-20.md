## Signal, not noise

**[How OpenAI Used Its Own LLMs to Design Its Jalapeño Chip](https://spectrum.ieee.org/llms-for-chip-design)** — *IEEE Spectrum*

The nine-month tape-out is the headline, but the mechanism is the story: OpenAI's engineers used their own models to explore the EDA design space, evaluate architectural trade-offs, and generate RTL optimizations faster than any human team could iterate. If this is repeatable — and a successful production chip suggests it is — the implication for the hardware supply chain is structural. Custom ASICs stop being an 18–24-month strategic bet and become a 9-month tactical weapon. Every well-capitalized lab will have this capability within a year, which makes the current GPU monoculture untenable by early 2027. The interesting second-order question is whether Nvidia accelerates its own design cycles in response, or whether the moat it thought it had simply evaporates.
