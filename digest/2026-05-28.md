# AI News Digest — 2026-05-28

**Executive summary.** The week's structural story is governance: Musk, Zuckerberg, and Sacks lobbied Trump into shelving a draft executive order that would have required pre-release federal safety testing of AI models — the first time the industry's informal anti-regulation bloc visibly stopped a White House initiative in its tracks. Capital concentration continued apace, with Anthropic set to close a second $30 billion round inside a single calendar year, this time at a $900 billion valuation that would eclipse OpenAI. On the product side, Google's AI-first Search redesign is generating the clearest consumer rejection signal to date: DuckDuckGo iOS installs peaked at 70% week-over-week growth as users looked for an exit.

---

**Top stories**

**1. Trump's AI safety order pulled after calls from Musk, Zuckerberg, and Sacks**
A draft White House executive order that would have asked frontier AI labs to voluntarily submit models for federal testing before release was killed last-minute; Musk later disputed his role, Meta disputed Zuckerberg's, and Sacks stayed quiet — but the order is dead regardless. *—[The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/trump-pulls-ai-safety-order-after-last-minute-calls-from-musk-zuckerberg-and-sacks/), [Semafor](https://www.semafor.com/article/05/21/2026/elon-musk-mark-zuckerberg-derail-trump-ai-order)*

**2. Anthropic closing second $30B round in 2026, valuation above $900B**
Sequoia, Dragoneer, Altimeter, and Greenoaks are each writing ~$2B checks in a round that would value Anthropic above $900 billion — roughly eight months after its February Series G at $380B; at this pace the denominator problem belongs to the investors, not the company. *—[Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-22/anthropic-to-close-over-30-billion-round-as-soon-as-next-week)*

**3. DuckDuckGo installs surge 30% after Google's AI Search overhaul**
Following Google I/O's conversion of Search into an AI-agent-first interface with no opt-out, DuckDuckGo reported U.S. iOS installs peaked at 69.9% week-over-week growth; the company's AI-free search page, noai.duckduckgo.com, averaged 22.7% WoW growth — a tidy measure of how many users want the 2004 web back. *—[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/26/duckduckgo-installs-are-up-30-as-users-reject-being-force-fed-googles-ai-search/)*

**4. BadHost (CVE-2026-48710): critical auth bypass in Starlette exposes entire Python AI stack**
A host-header normalization flaw in Starlette — 325 million weekly downloads, the ASGI foundation under FastAPI, vLLM, LiteLLM, and every MCP server — lets unauthenticated attackers route past protected paths; the patch (Starlette 1.0.1) shipped one day before public disclosure, giving operators near-zero lead time. *—[badhost.org](https://badhost.org/), [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48277107)*

**5. Thinking Machines announces full-duplex interaction model with 400ms latency**
Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab released TML-Interaction-Small, a "full-duplex" model that processes input and generates output simultaneously rather than turn-by-turn — functionally closer to a phone call than a chat window, with a claimed 0.40s response latency. *—[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/11/thinking-machines-wants-to-build-an-ai-that-actually-listens-while-it-talks/)*

**Signal, not noise**

BadHost deserves more attention than it is getting under the governance and funding headlines. Nearly every Python-based AI agent deployment — from self-hosted MCP servers to vLLM inference endpoints — runs Starlette somewhere in its dependency tree. The flaw is trivially exploitable: send a crafted Host header and skip authentication entirely. If you are running any FastAPI or MCP service exposed beyond localhost, check your Starlette version today.
