## Signal, not noise

**[Manifold Steering Reveals the Shared Geometry of Neural Network Representation and Behavior](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05115)** — *arXiv cs.LG / Goodfire AI*

Linear activation steering works by finding the right direction in representation space; this paper argues the premise is wrong. By fitting a curved activation manifold and steering along it instead, the authors achieve a 2.8x average improvement in naturalness across language and vision tasks — and explain why standard steering produces brittle, incoherent, off-target edits: it assumes flat geometry where the actual space is curved. If the result holds up at scale, the entire toolkit for controlling model behaviour — representation engineering, concept probing, activation patching — needs to be rethought in Riemannian terms. That's not a tweak; it's a new foundation for the field.
