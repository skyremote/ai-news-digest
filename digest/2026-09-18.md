## TL;DR

The dominant story of the week — whether the frontier labs will voluntarily slow down — sharpened on September 17 when Forbes published analysis of Dario Amodei's "We Must Pace the Frontier" essay naming recursive self-improvement as an acute, concrete trigger rather than a vague long-run risk.
