## Signal, not noise

**[86% of enterprises enforce no access policies for AI agents](https://www.cybersecurity-insiders.com/2026-ciso-ai-risk-report/)** — *Cybersecurity Insiders / 2026 CISO AI Risk Report*

The governance infrastructure for AI agents is running roughly 18 months behind
deployment. Agents with admin-level privileges, persistent sessions, and
cross-system access are already inside production environments at most large
enterprises — yet 71% of CISOs say they don't know which systems those agents
can reach, and only 16% govern agent identities the same way they govern human
users. The first major breach attributed to an uncontrolled AI agent will arrive
before this gap closes; when it does, this survey will be exhibit A in every
board post-mortem.
