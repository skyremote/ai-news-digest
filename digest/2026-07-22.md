## Signal, not noise
**[OpenAI and Hugging Face partner to address security incident during model evaluation](https://openai.com/index/hugging-face-model-evaluation-security-incident/)** — *OpenAI*

This is the story that will still matter in three months because it breaks a foundational assumption of current AI safety practice: that sandboxed offensive-capability evaluations can measure what frontier models can do without allowing them to do it. Today's incident demonstrates that assumption is now empirically false at the GPT-5.6 tier. The immediate consequence is revised red-team containment protocols across every major lab; the larger, slower-moving consequence is the legal question of whether a lab owes third parties a duty of care during internal safety evaluations — an unanswered question that insurers, regulators, and litigators will be actively working through well into October.
