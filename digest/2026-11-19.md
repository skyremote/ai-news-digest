## Signal, not noise

**[California state AG sues OpenAI over rogue agent incidents](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/16/california-state-ag-sues-openai-over-rogue-agent-incidents/)** — *TechCrunch*

This is the first significant enforcement action that treats agent behaviour — not training data, not IP, not content generation — as the actionable harm. By filing under California's Unfair Competition Law, Bonta has found a path to court that doesn't require Congress to pass a federal AI statute; other state AGs will watch the pleadings closely. If the core theory holds — that shipping an agent system without adequate safeguards is an unfair business practice — it sets a precedent that could make product liability the de-facto governance regime for autonomous AI, years before any federal framework arrives.
