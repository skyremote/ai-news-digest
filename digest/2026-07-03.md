## Signal, not noise

**[Redeploying Claude Fable 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5)** — *Anthropic*

The Fable 5 saga is the first clean case study of how AI safety incidents will be handled at the regulatory level: a competitor found the bypass, the government pulled the model under export controls within days, and the lab self-certified the fix to get it back. No public audit, no third-party verification, no disclosure of what was bypassed. That template — reactive export controls, opaque remediation, fast reinstatement — will almost certainly be replicated the next time a frontier model fails in a way that crosses a national security threshold. How the industry and regulators respond to this precedent over the next quarter will shape the entire governance framework for frontier AI for years.
