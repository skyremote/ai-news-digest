Google just made training and inference officially different hardware problems. The convention until now was a single accelerator family tuned at the software layer; the TPU 8t/8i split acknowledges that agentic inference — millions of concurrent short contexts, not batch throughput — has different memory locality, bandwidth, and latency requirements than pre-training runs. In three months this decision will pressure every hyperscaler to publish a similar training/inference taxonomy, and will force enterprise buyers to answer the hardware question before procurement conversations even begin; watch for the Nvidia response, or absence of one, to define the competitive silicon landscape for the next two years.
