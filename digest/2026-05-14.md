## Signal, not noise

**[AI Co-Mathematician: Accelerating Mathematicians with Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06651)** — *Google DeepMind*

Most AI progress stories are about benchmarks moving up and costs moving down. This one is different: a professional mathematician used the system interactively and closed a problem that had been open for sixty years. The 48% score on FrontierMath Tier 4 matters less than the workflow — asynchronous, stateful, tracking failed hypotheses — because that same loop applies to drug discovery, materials science, and any field where the hard work is navigating a vast conjecture space. In three months, watch for analogous systems showing up in protein-folding extensions and semiconductor-design verification; the Kourovka result is the proof of concept.
