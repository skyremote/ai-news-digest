## Signal, not noise

**[Google moves to make TPUs feel native to PyTorch as it targets Nvidia's CUDA advantage](https://techinformed.com/google-moves-to-make-tpus-feel-native-to-pytorch-as-it-targets-nvidias-cuda-advantage/)** — *TechInformed*

CUDA's moat has never really been the hardware — it has been the decade of developer tooling, op-level optimisations, and muscle memory baked into every open-source model codebase. TorchTPU and TorchNeuron land in the same week and change the frame: if the same PyTorch code runs on TPUs and Trainium without rewrites, the hardware choice becomes a pure price-performance decision. The next frontier training run will be planned against Nvidia, TPU v7, and Trainium 3 cost curves simultaneously in a way that was not practically possible twelve months ago. That repricing pressure arrives in accelerator contract negotiations over the next quarter, and it arrives with working code, not a roadmap.
