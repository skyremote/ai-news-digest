## Signal, not noise

**[Our eighth generation TPUs: two chips for the agentic era](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/eighth-generation-tpu-agentic-era/)** — *Google Blog*

Every hyperscaler until now has shipped chips that handle training and inference on the same architecture with different tuning knobs. Google splitting the 8th generation into two distinct products is the first public acknowledgment that agentic inference — millions of concurrent low-latency requests with large KV caches — is structurally incompatible with training's need for massive batch throughput and inter-chip bandwidth. In three months, when the conversation shifts from "can we build agents?" to "how do we run them at production cost?", the TPU 8t/8i split will be the reference architecture — and the pricing model it enables will set the floor that Nvidia has to beat.
