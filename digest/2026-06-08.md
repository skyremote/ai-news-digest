## Signal, not noise

**[Federal AI bill would freeze state laws for three years](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317903/20260606/federal-ai-regulation-bill-freezes-state-consumer-protections-three-years-sparks-revolt.htm)** — *TechTimes*

If the Great American AI Act passes substantially as drafted, it wipes out every state-level AI consumer-protection law for three years and replaces them with a single federal audit regime — handing Congress, not California or Colorado, the pen during AI's most consequential regulatory formation period. The three-year sunset is political cover; the structural effect is to clear the field while the EU AI Act generates binding precedent that US companies must comply with regardless. Any founder building AI products in the US should treat this as a two-scenario planning problem: a federal preemption era where one audit framework governs everything, or a fragmented multi-state patchwork if the bill stalls — because in three months one of those realities will be much more likely than it is today.
