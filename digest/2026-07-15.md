## Signal, not noise

**[Grok Build Uploaded Entire Git Repositories to xAI Storage, Not Just Files It Read](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/grok-build-git-repository-upload/)** — *Cybernews*

Researcher cereblab's traffic analysis exposed something structurally important: the Grok Build CLI was not leaking data through a bug, it was uploading whole repositories as a first-class feature — then reading a small slice of them for inference. The 27,800× gap between bytes consumed and bytes uploaded is not a rounding error; it is an architectural choice. Every AI coding tool that needs "project context" faces the same temptation, and most have not told users exactly what leaves the machine. The question to ask your coding assistant vendor is not "do you store my code?" but "how much more do you upload than you read?" — and the absence of a clear answer is now a red flag, not a minor omission.
