# AI News Digest — 2026-05-07

OpenAI pushed GPT-5.5 Instant to all ChatGPT users, claiming a 52.5% reduction in hallucinations on high-stakes prompts and an AIME 2025 score jump from 65.4 to 81.2, while Google's Gemini 3.1 Ultra with a 2-million-token context window continues rolling out — the model cadence is now faster than any reasonable evaluation cycle can track. ## Top 5 Stories

**1. OpenAI ships GPT-5.5 Instant as ChatGPT's new default**

The model replaces GPT-5.3 Instant across all tiers, producing responses 30% shorter while cutting hallucinated claims by 52.5% on medicine, law, and finance prompts; personalization from connected Gmail and past chats is rolling out to Plus and Pro users. _—[OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-5-instant/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/05/openai-releases-gpt-5-5-instant-a-new-default-model-for-chatgpt/)_

**2. White House blocks Mythos expansion; Pentagon formalises Anthropic blacklist**

Claude Mythos — a restricted frontier model that autonomously discovered a 27-year-old OpenBSD vulnerability — is confined to roughly 120 vetted organisations under "Project Glasswing"; the Trump administration objects to both the controlled rollout pace and Anthropic's refusal to permit domestic surveillance or lethal autonomous-weapons use. _—[The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2026/05/01/mythos_complicates_anthropic_us_gov_breakup/), [Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/white-house-opposes-anthropic-mythos-expansion)_

**3. Pentagon clears eight firms for classified AI networks; Anthropic stays out**

AWS, Google, Microsoft, Nvidia, OpenAI, Oracle, Reflection AI, and SpaceX signed agreements to deploy AI at Impact Level 6/7, explicitly accelerating the DoD's pivot away from Anthropic after the two sides deadlocked over "any lawful use" terms. _—[TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/01/pentagon-inks-deals-with-nvidia-microsoft-and-aws-to-deploy-ai-on-classified-networks/), [Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2026/05/pentagon-clears-7-tech-firms-to-deploy-their-ai-on-its-classified-networks/)_

**4. Stanford AI Index 2026: 29.6 GW, China at 2.7% gap, Anthropic overtakes OpenAI on ARR**

The annual index finds US AI data centers collectively draw as much power as New York State at peak, China has nearly erased the US performance lead while spending a fraction of the capital, and — separately from the index — Anthropic's ARR hit $30B against OpenAI's $24B for the first time. _—[Stanford HAI](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/inside-the-ai-index-12-takeaways-from-the-2026-report), [Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/stanford-ai-index-2026-china-us-performance-gap)_

**5. Google Gemini 3.1 Ultra rolls out with 2M-token context**

The model ingests roughly 3,000 pages of dense text or one hour of video in a single prompt, ships sandboxed code execution and native multimodal reasoning, and is available via Gemini Advanced, AI Studio, and the Gemini API. _—[Google Cloud Docs](https://docs.cloud.google.com/vertex-ai/generative-ai/docs/models/gemini/3-1-pro)_

## Signal-Not-Noise

**ARIS: Autonomous Research via Adversarial Multi-Agent Collaboration** _—[arXiv 2605.03042](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03042)_

While the model-release noise dominates the feed, this paper from Shanghai Jiao Tong is worth actual attention. ARIS is an open-source harness (8k+ GitHub stars already) that runs a Claude executor against a GPT reviewer in an adversarial loop across five ML research workflows — idea discovery, experiment, review, manuscript, rebuttal — connected by plain-text artifact contracts. The architectural bet is that cross-model adversarial review catches the correlated errors that same-model self-refinement structurally cannot. Average manuscript quality scores rose from 5.0 to 7.5/10 in internal evals. The interesting question is not whether the system works today, but whether this pattern — using model-family diversity as an error-correction mechanism — becomes the default scaffolding for agentic research infrastructure. If it does, the implications for research monoculture run in both directions.
