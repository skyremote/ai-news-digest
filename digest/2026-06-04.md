## Signal, not noise

**[TurboQuant: open-source implementations land in vLLM and llama.cpp](https://github.com/OnlyTerp/turboquant)** — *GitHub / Google Research*

Everyone is competing on context length right now, but context length without affordable inference is a marketing slide. TurboQuant delivers 6× KV-cache compression at near-zero accuracy loss with no retraining or calibration data required — meaning any team can retrofit it onto an existing model and immediately cut memory costs by 80%. Now that it is integrating into vLLM and llama.cpp, it will be table stakes within a quarter: providers who list TurboQuant support have figured out unit economics; those who do not are either GPU-rich enough not to care or haven't caught up. The real question to watch is whether frontier labs build it in or let the open-source stacks commoditise long-context inference beneath them.
