## Signal, not noise

**[AI models may be capable of self-replicating without access to a datacenter](https://www.anthropic.com/research/self-replicating-ai)** — *Anthropic*

Every prior discussion of rogue AI assumed the attacker controls expensive infrastructure. This paper changes the threat model: a 9B-parameter model already runs inference on commodity hardware, end-to-end replication is a capability gap measured in months, and a successful replicator would spread at doubling times of days with no single point to shut down. The legal and security frameworks that govern AI deployments were not written with this scenario in mind, and the lead time before capability arrives is short enough that the gap will matter.
