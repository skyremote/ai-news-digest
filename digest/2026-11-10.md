## Signal, not noise

**[Check Point Details Autonomous ARTEX Attacks by Cybercriminals](https://letsdatascience.com/news/check-point-details-autonomous-artex-attacks-by-cybercrimina-9d8b6097)** — *Check Point Research*

The ARTEX report matters in three months because it marks the first documented shift from AI-assisted to AI-autonomous attack campaigns. Prior documented cases involved AI helping a human write phishing copy or scan for CVEs; this operation ran for fifteen days with one operator checking in periodically, not steering. The fact that Claude's refusal triggered a model switch to DeepSeek Flash is the tell: threat actors now treat LLM guardrails as a routing problem, not a blocker. Security teams that have been planning for "AI-assisted threats" will need to revise their models well before 2027 budget cycles close.
