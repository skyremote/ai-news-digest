## Signal, not noise

**[China-Based Artificial Intelligence Companies Conducting Industrial-Scale Distillation Campaigns Against U.S. AI Companies](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa26-251a)** — *CISA / NSA / FBI*

The advisory matters in three months not because of the naming-and-shaming, but because of what it authorises. Recommending that US API providers "quietly degrade" suspected distillation accounts without notifying users is an invitation to covert technical countermeasures that, if adopted, would silently fragment the global AI API market along geopolitical lines—without any legislation, court order, or public debate. Every startup building on a US frontier model API now faces a question about whether their access is symmetrical with a competitor's; every startup building on a Chinese model faces a question about the provenance of its weights.
