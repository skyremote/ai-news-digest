## Signal, not noise

**[The AI Scientist-v2: Workshop-Level Automated Scientific Discovery via Agentic Tree Search](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08066)** — *arXiv (SakanaAI)*

Most AI-in-science coverage focuses on assistants that help researchers write or search. AI Scientist-v2 is different: it closes the loop entirely—hypothesis, experiment, analysis, manuscript, submission—and one of its papers cleared the peer-review bar at an ICLR workshop without human intervention. The venue is modest and the system's output will be contested, but the proof-of-concept matters structurally: if autonomous research pipelines reach even modest reliability, they change the economics of scientific discovery by decoupling throughput from headcount. Three months from now, the question will not be whether this happened but how many labs are running similar pipelines quietly.
