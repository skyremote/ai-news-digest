# AI Digest — 2026-05-24

## TL;DR

The US government effectively exited federal AI oversight when Trump killed a draft executive order on frontier model testing after eleventh-hour industry lobbying, leaving the field with no coherent federal baseline. Against that regulatory vacuum, AI economics crossed a structural milestone: Anthropic is projecting its first-ever operating profit and raising at a $900 billion valuation, vindicating enterprise coding tools as a repeatable business model rather than a burn-rate bet. The same 24-hour window produced the week's harsher signals—Meta fired 8,000 employees whose own keystrokes it had been harvesting as training data, and a supply-chain attack via a poisoned VS Code extension gave an outside threat actor access to GitHub's internal repositories.

## Top 5

1. **[Trump abruptly kills landmark AI executive order](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/trump-scraps-signing-landmark-executive-order-regulating-ai-rcna346288)** — *NBC News* — Last-minute calls from David Sacks and industry allies persuaded Trump to shelve a draft EO that would have established voluntary cybersecurity and frontier-model testing requirements; the US now has no active federal AI oversight mechanism.

2. **[Anthropic in talks to raise at $900B valuation, projects first-ever operating profit](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-revenue-explosive-growth-ipo-profitable-quarter.html)** — *CNBC* — Q2 revenue is expected to reach $10.9B with a $559M operating profit, driven almost entirely by Claude Code; a funding round above $900B—surpassing OpenAI—is expected to close as early as next week ahead of an October IPO.

3. **[Meta surveilled employees' Gmail, keystrokes, and code to train AI—then fired 8,000](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/18/metas-layoffs-starting-this-week-underscore-zuckerbergs-ai-reality-.html)** — *CNBC* — The internal "Model Capability Initiative" logged mouse movements and screenshots on company devices to build training data; over 1,000 employees signed a protest petition before the cuts proceeded.

4. **[Intuit cuts 3,000 jobs—17% of its workforce—to fund AI restructuring](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/20/intuit-to-lay-off-over-3000-employees-to-refocus-on-ai/)** — *TechCrunch* — QuickBooks and TurboTax parent sheds headcount across Reno and Woodland Hills while doubling down on AI agents; the CEO's "nothing to do with AI" framing landed awkwardly given the simultaneous pivot announcement.

5. **[GitHub's internal repositories breached via 18-minute poisoned VS Code extension](https://cyberscoop.com/github-internal-repositories-vs-code-extension-attack/)** — *CyberScoop* — A trojanized version of Nx Console (2.2M installs) was live on the VS Code Marketplace for 18 minutes and exfiltrated credentials from roughly 3,800 GitHub internal repos; root cause traces to a prior TanStack npm supply-chain compromise, meaning one upstream dependency poisoned two downstream victims.

## Signal, not noise

**[Anthropic in talks to raise at $900B valuation, projects first-ever operating profit](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/20/anthropic-revenue-explosive-growth-ipo-profitable-quarter.html)** — *CNBC*

The Anthropic profitability inflection matters more than the valuation number. When a frontier safety-focused lab crosses into operating profit at this scale—$559M on roughly $10.9B revenue—it dismantles the argument that responsible-AI constraints are a drag on commercial returns. More specifically, it identifies the product that breaks the mold: Claude Code, an agentic coding tool that hit $1B annualised revenue within six months of launch. That single data point will redirect the next wave of AI infrastructure investment toward agentic developer tooling, and any lab or startup not already positioned there is now playing catch-up.
