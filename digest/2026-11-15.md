## Signal, not noise

**[Anthropic's FlashProve: cryptographic proof of AI model identity](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/anthropic-is-working-on-a-system-to-prove-ai-models-identity/)** — *TechCrunch*

As AI agents proliferate and orchestration stacks grow more opaque, the question of *which model is actually running* becomes a legal and security issue, not just an engineering curiosity. FlashProve is the first working prototype that lets a user verify model identity against a cryptographic fingerprint of the weights — without the lab revealing those weights. If a lightweight verification path lands in production API responses, it becomes the foundation for model SLAs, procurement compliance, and regulatory auditing. The proof-generation cost is still prohibitive at scale, but that is an engineering problem with a clear path; the hard part — the cryptographic construction — is now published and open.
