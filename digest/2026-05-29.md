## Signal, not noise

**[Model Capacity Determines Grokking through Competing Memorisation and Generalisation Speeds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09724)** — *ArXiv cs.LG*

Grokking — delayed generalisation after apparent overfitting — has been one of the most reproducible yet unexplained findings in modern deep learning. This paper supplies a mechanism: generalisation wins only once the model has enough capacity to compete on the generalisation timescale, not merely the memorisation one. If the framework scales beyond modular arithmetic, it hands practitioners a predictive handle on when "suddenly getting it" will and won't occur, and could inform training-budget decisions the way the bias-variance tradeoff once informed classical model selection.
