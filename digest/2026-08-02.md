Today's activation matters not just as a compliance checkbox but as the start of enforcement precedent. The AI Office now has standing to issue fines, and the first cases it chooses to pursue — likely high-visibility chatbots or synthetic-media platforms — will set the floor for what "adequate disclosure" means in practice. Companies that have been treating watermarking as aspirational now have three months to show inspectors something real before the first enforcement wave hits. The simultaneous California SB 942 activation closes a dual-jurisdiction gap that had let many US-headquartered products delay; a product compliant in Brussels after today is not automatically compliant in Sacramento, and vice versa.
