## Signal, not noise

**[From AGI to ASI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12683)** — *arXiv / Google DeepMind*

Most AGI discourse collapses the question of "what comes after AGI" into either science fiction or hand-waving. This paper, from 14 DeepMind researchers, does the opposite: it tries to formally characterise the technical pathways connecting AGI-level systems (defined as roughly median-human performance across cognitive tasks) to systems that surpass the aggregate cognitive output of humanity. The argument that the transition may arrive through "a series of transformative societal changes" rather than a single recursive step is not reassuring — it means the governance problems compound incrementally before any single alarm sounds. Expect this paper to anchor safety proposals and regulatory language for the next 12 months in the same way DeepMind's 2022 AGI safety framework did; it will be the citation that grounds every subsequent policy brief.
